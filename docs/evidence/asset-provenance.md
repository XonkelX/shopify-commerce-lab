## Prompt

> Create one wide 3:1 ecommerce product image containing three equal square panels arranged left to right. Each panel must have the same seamless warm off-white studio background, identical soft diffused daylight, matching camera angle, generous margins, and a subtle soft shadow. Left panel: one compact 20-ounce matte sage-green stainless-steel insulated bottle with a short rounded silhouette and simple screw cap. Center panel: one 24-ounce matte terracotta stainless-steel insulated bottle with a medium-height balanced silhouette and simple screw cap. Right panel: one large 32-ounce matte deep-navy stainless-steel insulated bottle with a tall robust silhouette, simple screw cap, and discreet carry loop. Clean premium photorealistic ecommerce packshots. Keep each complete bottle centered within its own third so the three panels can be cropped into separate square assets. No logos, no text, no hands, no props, no watermark, and no borders between panels.
