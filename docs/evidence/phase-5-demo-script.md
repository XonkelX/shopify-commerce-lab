# Phase 5 Purchase Logic Demo Script

Target length: 60–75 seconds.

1. Open the authenticated [case calculator Theme Editor preview](https://admin.shopify.com/store/oniel-lab/themes/155175092398/editor?previewPath=%2Fproducts%2Fatlas-insulated-bottle%3Fview%3Dcase-calculator&previewMode=mobile).
2. Show the mobile-friendly product context, real Color/Size variants, and merchant explanation that one Shopify quantity represents one 12-bottle case.
3. Enter 120 bottles with 0% reserve. Point out the exact 10-case result, 120-bottle capacity, and zero remaining.
4. Change the request to 121. Show the predictable ceiling to 11 cases and the complete equation.
5. Enter 125 bottles and 10% reserve. Show 137.5 planned bottles, 11.46 raw cases, 12 purchase cases, 144 capacity, and 6.5 remaining.
6. Enter `12.5`, `2001`, and `51%` briefly to demonstrate whole-unit, maximum, and reserve-range validation.
7. Select Sage / 32 oz to show the real sold-out variant and disabled action, then select Navy / 32 oz.
8. Point out the $40 per-case Shopify price and $480 estimate for 12 cases.
9. Submit the native Shopify form.
10. In cart, show Navy / 32 oz, quantity 12, $480 subtotal, and the four calculation properties: requested 125, reserve 10%, 12 bottles per case, and the full ceiling equation.
11. Open the Theme Editor settings to show merchant-editable units per case, defaults, maximum, labels, note, and palette.
12. Close on the architecture: transparent browser calculation, whole-unit Shopify quantity, native form submission, and Shopify-authoritative cart pricing.
